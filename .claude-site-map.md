## Gotchas / Past Bugs
- **2026-07-06 — new SEO posts must only reference assets and slugs that exist.** The Jul 6 screen posts shipped with `heroImage: /img/hero-screen-cleaning.jpg` (never existed; the screen-post hero is `/img/water_pole_2.jpg`) and an internal link to a fabricated slug `/blog/window-cleaning-ventura-keeping-coastal-glass-clean` — 7 Semrush errors, site health 96%→92%. Before committing a post: check `heroImage` against `public/img/` and every `/blog/...` link against `src/content/blog/` filenames.
- **No em dashes in post content** (title, description, body, FAQ JSON-LD) — hard content rule; the Jul 6 posts violated it and were swept.
- **Em/en dash sweep status (2026-07-24).** The 07-15 and 07-20 runs only cleaned
  `metaTitle`/`metaDescription`, leaving dashes in the rendered *body* copy of every page.
  - **DONE**: all of `src/pages/*.astro` (all 10 city pages, `index`, `privacy`) plus
    `CityLanding.astro`, `Base.astro`, `CookieBanner.astro`. Every rendered page outside `/blog`
    is now dash-clean; verified against `dist/`.
  - **REMAINING: ~700 dashes across 30 files in `src/content/blog/`.** Deliberately not swept in one
    pass, because this needs sentence-by-sentence judgement, not search-and-replace. Do it a few
    posts at a time, ideally when a post is being refreshed for another reason.
  - **When sweeping, never blind-replace an em dash with a comma.** It silently produces comma splices
    ("Salt is hygroscopic, it pulls moisture"). Correct targets vary: a colon before a list or an
    explanation, a period between two independent clauses, "and"/"because" where the logical link
    matters, and a plain comma only for a true appositive. Numeric ranges (`$175-$425`, `4-6`,
    `Feb-April`) become "to".
