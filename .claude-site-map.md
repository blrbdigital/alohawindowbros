## Pages (30 total)
- `/` — Home (hero, stats, wave dividers, how it works, reviews, services, guarantees, areas, FAQ, CTA, contact, footer)
  - 4 SVG wave dividers between sections (blue→purple gradients matching truck branding)
- `/privacy` — Privacy policy. Required for Meta Ads compliance (Pixel disclosure, CCPA, opt-out paths). Reuses `blog-post-hero` + `service-content-section` layout.
- `/blog` — Blog listing
- `/blog/:slug` — 20 blog posts (content collection)
- `/services/:slug` — 3 service pages (window-washing, screen-cleaning, solar-panel-cleaning)
- `/thousand-oaks`, `/westlake`, `/agoura`, `/newbury` — City landing pages (use CityLanding component). These URLs preserve backlink equity from the original WordPress city pages (Google had them indexed). Each has unique soiling factors, neighborhood list, and FAQ — distinct from the longer-form blog guide that lives under /blog/.

## SEO
- Per-page title, description, canonical, Open Graph
- Titles kept <60 chars (page <title> drops brand suffix; brand stays in OG, JSON-LD, header logo)
- JSON-LD: LocalBusiness (home + cities), Article + BreadcrumbList (blog), Service + BreadcrumbList (services), FAQPage (city pages)
- Sitemap at /sitemap-index.xml (legacy `/sitemap.xml` 301s to current index)
- `/llms.txt` — AI-search index file pointing at services, cities, and guides
- 301 redirects from old WordPress URLs (blog, service, city .html, /index, /service, /schedule)
- 301 redirect for www → non-www (Host: www.alohawindowbros.com server block)
- 410 Gone for wp-admin, wp-login, wp-content, wp-includes, wp-json, xmlrpc, wp-*.php
- Nginx gzip enabled (text/css, JS, JSON, XML, SVG) — fixes "uncompressed JS/CSS" Semrush warning
- HSTS header (max-age=31536000, includeSubDomains)
