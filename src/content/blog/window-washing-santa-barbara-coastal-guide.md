## Why Window Washing in Santa Barbara Is Different

Santa Barbara sits between the Pacific and the Santa Ynez Mountains, so most homes deal with marine air and hillside conditions within a few miles of each other. The main residues on Santa Barbara glass are:

- **Salt film:** marine layer and onshore breezes leave a salty haze that hardens if it sits for months, heaviest on ocean-facing glass.
- **Hard-water spots:** sprinkler overspray dries into white mineral rings, and the water here carries a lot of minerals (see the next section).
- **Oak pollen:** coast live oaks in Mission Canyon, Hope Ranch, and the Riviera drop heavy spring pollen.
- **Wildfire ash:** fine ash from fires in the Santa Ynez front range settles on glass and screens long after the smoke clears.

## How Hard Is Santa Barbara's Water?

It depends on which agency serves your street, and the numbers are high. Each district below publishes hardness in its own water quality report:

| Water agency | Area served | Published hardness |
|---|---|---|
| Montecito Water District | Montecito, Summerland | Surface water 19 to 26 gpg, groundwater 19 to 38 gpg ([2024 report](https://www.montecitowater.com/doc/ccr2024/)) |
| Goleta Water District | Goleta and the Goleta Valley | Averages 20 gpg ([water quality report](https://www.goletawater.com/doc/18955/)) |
| Carpinteria Valley Water District | Carpinteria | 17 to 22 gpg ([district FAQ](https://www.cvwd.net/water-quality-faq)) |
| City of Santa Barbara | The city itself | No single figure; the supply mix changes by water year |

For comparison, Westlake Village's utilities report about 8.5 grains per gallon, so homes around Santa Barbara can be dealing with two to four times the mineral load on every drop of overspray.

The City of Santa Barbara is the exception because its water comes from many places. In water year 2025, its [Water Supply Management Report](https://santabarbaraca.gov/sites/default/files/2026-02/2025_Water_Supply_Mgmt_Report.pdf) shows 38% from Gibraltar Reservoir, 26% from Lake Cachuma, 17% from the Charles E. Meyer Desalination Plant, 10% from Mission Tunnel, and the rest recycled water, with no State Water Project water delivered that year. Because that blend is rebuilt every year, the mineral content moves with it.

## Window Washing vs. Window Cleaning

Homeowners use the terms interchangeably, and so do we: it is the same complete service. Here is how it compares with the one service that really is different:

| Service | Scope | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Window washing or window cleaning | Exterior and interior glass, screens, frames, tracks, and sills | Regular maintenance, move-ins, listings, after storms |
| Hard water restoration | Polishing mineral deposits out of etched glass, quoted separately at $200 to $500 | Haze that stays after a proper cleaning |

Restoration is not an acid wash. The glass industry's cleaning standard says "glass cleaning solutions should not include acids," and our [hard water spot removal guide](/blog/how-to-remove-hard-water-spots-from-windows/) explains how to tell a removable spot from etching before you pay for the wrong service.

## The Professional Window Washing Process

1. **Site walk-through:** we note cracked panes, failed seals, and hard-water hot spots before any water touches the glass.
2. **Landscape protection:** delicate plants and outdoor furniture are covered.
3. **Exterior wash with purified water:** a water-fed pole lifts salt and mineral residue, and the final rinse is purified water so no new spots dry on. [Unger](https://usa.ungerglobal.com/blog/10-common-mistakes-in-pure-water-window-cleaning/), which makes pure-water systems, says rinse water above **10 ppm** of total dissolved solids may not give a spot-free finish.
4. **Interior squeegee detail:** hand-finished edges, drop cloths where needed.
5. **Screen wash and reinstall:** each screen washed, dried, and checked for tears.
6. **Frame and track wipe-down:** salt and pollen cleared from tracks so windows slide freely.
7. **Final walk-through:** nothing is done until you have looked at it with us.
