Westlake Village homeowners assume their window spotting is caused by unusually hard water. The published numbers say otherwise. Both water systems serving Westlake Village test at an average of 146 milligrams per liter of calcium carbonate, which works out to roughly 8.5 grains per gallon, only about half the hardness of Ojai's local well water. The spotting is real, but the cause is not an extreme mineral concentration. It is how often that water lands on the glass and how fast it dries there. Aloha Window Bros provides professional [window washing](/services/window-washing) and hard water restoration throughout [Westlake Village](/westlake/), and on most Westlake homes the fix has less to do with the water and more to do with the sprinklers.

## What Is Actually in Westlake Village Water

Westlake Village is split across two water utilities, which is why homeowners on opposite sides of the same neighborhood sometimes get different answers. Remarkably, both land on the same number.

California Water Service supplies the Ventura County portion. Its [Westlake district water quality report](https://www.calwater.com/ccrs/wlk-wlk-2023/) lists total hardness averaging 146 ppm with a range of 138 to 153 ppm, and states that the district purchases 100% of its surface water from the Metropolitan Water District of Southern California through Calleguas Municipal Water District, with no groundwater wells at all.

The Las Virgenes Municipal Water District supplies the Los Angeles County side. Its [water quality report](https://www.lvmwd.gov/water-quality-report) lists total hardness averaging 146 mg/L with a range of 137 to 157 mg/L, and states plainly that the district "is entirely dependent upon water imported from elsewhere; there are no local drinking water sources." That water originates at Lake Oroville in the Sierra Nevada and travels down the State Water Project.

> "Both water systems serving Westlake Village average exactly 146 as calcium carbonate, roughly 8.5 grains per gallon. Because neither utility pumps local groundwater, Westlake Village homes never see the mineral load that well water communities in Ventura County live with every day."

To put that in context, the [U.S. Geological Survey](https://www.usgs.gov/special-topics/water-science-school/science/hardness-water) classifies water above 121 mg/L as hard and above 180 mg/L as very hard. At 146, Westlake Village water sits in the lower half of the hard band. California Water Service labels it "moderately hard" in its own report.

## So Why Do Westlake Windows Spot So Badly?

Because hardness is only one of the two variables. The other is evaporation, and Westlake Village is stacked against you there.

A mineral deposit forms when water lands on glass and then evaporates in place. The mineral concentration determines how much residue each drop leaves. The number of drops and how fast they dry determine how quickly that residue stacks up into a visible stain. Moderately hard water applied to a window two hundred times a summer will absolutely ruin it.

Three things drive that in Westlake Village:

- **Sprinkler overspray.** This is the dominant cause. Westlake's landscaping standards mean heavy, frequent irrigation on well kept lots, and spray heads set close to the house clip windows and stucco every single cycle.
- **Lake humidity and morning marine layer.** Moisture condenses on cool glass overnight and picks up airborne dust and mineral residue, then dries into a film as the sun hits it.
- **Afternoon sun on south and west elevations.** Fast evaporation is what turns a drop of water into a bonded deposit. Water that dries slowly leaves far less behind.

> "A Westlake Village window hit by sprinkler overspray three times a week receives more than 150 mineral depositing events per irrigation season. The water is moderately hard. The frequency is what makes it destructive."

This is genuinely good news for homeowners, because frequency is something you control. Mineral concentration is not.

## The Damage Timeline

Hard water damage on glass is progressive, and the last stage is not reversible.

| Deposit age | What is happening | Removal outlook |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 60 days | Minerals sit loose on the surface | Comes off completely |
| 2 to 6 months | Deposits harden and bond to the surface | 90 to 100% with a mineral dissolver |
| 6 to 12 months | Layer thickens, haze becomes visible | 70 to 95% with professional polishing |
| Over 12 months | Minerals bond chemically with the silica | Etched, often permanent |

Because Westlake water is only moderately hard, homes here move through that timeline more slowly than Ojai homes do. That is exactly why Westlake spotting so often goes unaddressed until it has etched. Nothing looks urgent until suddenly the glass is permanently cloudy.

> "Etching is not a stain on the window, it is damage in the window. Once minerals have bonded with the silica, no cleaning product will restore clarity. The options at that point are professional polishing, which recovers most but not all of it, or replacing the pane at $300 to $800."

## Fixing It: Sprinklers First, Glass Second

Cleaning the windows without fixing the overspray means paying to clean them again in eight weeks. Aloha Window Bros recommends doing these in order.

1. **Walk your irrigation zones while they run.** Actually stand outside during a cycle. Most homeowners have never seen which heads are hitting the house, and it is almost always one or two.
2. **Rotate or replace the offenders.** Swap fixed spray heads for rotators or drip in beds along the walls. This single change eliminates the majority of hard water staining on most Westlake homes.
3. **Water before sunrise.** Cooler air means slower evaporation, and slower evaporation means less mineral residue left behind on anything the water touches.
4. **Do not hose the windows down.** Rinsing with tap water and letting it air dry deposits a fresh layer of minerals. You are not rinsing the problem off, you are reapplying it.
5. **Then restore the glass,** using purified water with the minerals filtered out so the final rinse dries leaving nothing behind.

For light, recent spotting, a 1:1 mix of distilled white vinegar and warm water will handle it. Let it dwell five to ten minutes, never let it dry on the pane, and never work in direct sun. Skip steel wool and scouring pads entirely, since the micro scratches they leave hold minerals better than smooth glass does.

## How Often Westlake Village Homes Should Be Cleaned

Aloha Window Bros recommends professional cleaning every four to six months for most Westlake Village homes, versus every three to four months in the harder water communities like Ojai. Homes with sprinklers that hit the glass, or lakefront homes exposed to constant overnight moisture, should stay at the four month end.

The point of a schedule is to keep deposits in the "rinses right off" stage. Cleaning when the windows finally look bad means cleaning after the minerals have already bonded, which costs more and recovers less.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How hard is the water in Westlake Village?

Westlake Village water averages 146 as calcium carbonate, roughly 8.5 grains per gallon, in both water systems that serve the community. California Water Service reports 146 ppm with a range of 138 to 153 ppm for its Westlake district, and Las Virgenes Municipal Water District reports 146 mg/L with a range of 137 to 157 mg/L. The U.S. Geological Survey classifies that as hard but well below the very hard threshold of 180 mg/L.

### Who supplies water to Westlake Village?

It depends which side of the county line you are on. California Water Service supplies the Ventura County portion, purchasing 100% of its supply from the Metropolitan Water District through Calleguas Municipal Water District. Las Virgenes Municipal Water District supplies the Los Angeles County side with imported State Water Project water. Neither utility uses local groundwater wells, which is why Westlake hardness is far lower than in well water communities like Ojai.

### If the water is only moderately hard, why are my windows spotted?

Because staining is driven by how often water lands on the glass and how fast it dries, not just by mineral concentration. Sprinkler overspray in Westlake Village can hit a window well over 150 times in an irrigation season, and each cycle leaves residue behind. Moderately hard water applied that frequently, then flash dried by afternoon sun, will build a bonded deposit. Redirecting the sprinklers usually matters more than the hardness number does.

### Can I remove hard water stains from my Westlake Village windows myself?

Fresh spotting under about 60 days old usually comes off with a 1:1 distilled white vinegar and warm water solution, applied out of direct sun and never allowed to dry on the glass. Deposits older than roughly six months typically require professional polishing compounds. Past 12 months, minerals may have etched the glass permanently, at which point cleaning cannot restore it.

### Are hard water stains permanent?

Not if they are caught in time. Deposits under 12 months old can usually be fully or nearly fully restored. Beyond that, the minerals bond chemically with the silica in the glass and the result is etching, which no cleaner will remove.
